Our Story

A Taste of
Shiraz in Toronto

Nestled in the heart of Midtown Toronto, City Kebab brings the warmth and flavors of authentic Persian cuisine to Yonge Street. Every dish is crafted with heart — the kind of homemade quality you'd find in a traditional Persian home.

"The food was truly homemade quality — after many years, I finally tasted real Persian food again."

From our signature saffron-marinated kebabs to our legendary homemade doogh — voted best in Canada by our guests — every visit is a celebration of Persian hospitality and culture.
